v0.7.1 fixes the layout and touch problems of the NAS on phones and tablets. Nothing changes on the server, so the upgrade needs no new setting.

---

## 1. Fixes for phones and tablets

(commits `ca8c60c`, `69c6c06`, `09eaf4f`, `fb9b21a`, `f9d3abb`, `ee5c3f0`, `af796c8`, `adedf99`, `da62636`, `75f090f`, `ba9dd62`, `303de4d`, `723cdbb`, `24338c6`)

**Viewers.**

- Images and PDFs open at a zoom that fits the screen. Before, they opened at 100%, the left and top parts were cut off, and scrolling could not reach them. The zoom label no longer breaks into two lines.
- The video speed menu is no longer covered by the center play button, and it scrolls inside the player on short landscape screens. The progress bar and the volume bar of video and audio have a touch height of 44 px instead of 8 px. A video that cannot play shows only the error notice.
- The audio player keeps its title and volume row inside short landscape screens.

**File explorer on touch screens.**

- On touch screens 768 px and wider (landscape phones, tablets), one tap opens a file, and the more button, the Select item, and the multi-select bar are shown, as on narrow phones.
- A long press no longer opens the item or closes the menu it just opened when the finger is lifted.
- On landscape screens 500 px high or less, the folder title row is hidden, so the list shows more rows.
- In deep folders, the path row scrolls to show the current folder.

**Layout.**

- Menus and the share-link dialog stay inside the screen and scroll inside when they are taller than the screen.
- In Quick Open, a long file name no longer pushes the path out of view.
- On phones whose address bar is visible, the bottom of the sign-in page is no longer hidden.
- Placeholder text in narrow input fields ends with an ellipsis instead of being cut.
- With many tabs, the active tab scrolls into view.

**Touch targets and overlap.**

- The tab close button, the permission switches, and the header search are at least 44 px for touch.
- Notifications no longer cover the buttons of the item menu and of the upload sheet, and only a limited number are shown at once.

## 2. Other fixes

(commits `58f09d6`, `64b7f41`, `ec41801`)

- **Activity log.** When two records of the same kind had the same millisecond, the activity page stopped at "Loading…". It now shows them. Deleting or uploading several files at once can create such records.
- **Colors.** The dim layer behind menus and dialogs and the video background now use Signal tokens. The dim layer looks the same. The video background is `#090a0d` instead of `#000`, the same as the image and code viewers.
- **Reduced motion.** With reduced motion on, moving to a PDF page jumps instead of scrolling smoothly.

---

## 3. Upgrade notes

- **One tap opens files on wide touch screens.** On touch screens 768 px and wider, a tap now opens the file. Use the more button or Select to select without opening.
- **Images and PDFs open fitted.** Use the zoom controls to see them at 100%.
- **Short landscape screens.** The folder title row with the item count is hidden when the screen is 500 px high or less.
- **No server change.** The server, the API, the database, and the settings are the same as in v0.7.0.

---

## 4. Known limits

- The fixes were checked with touch emulation in WebKit, not on real iPhone or iPad devices. Address-bar resizing, long-press handling, and safe areas should be checked on a device.
- New folder, rename, and delete still use the browser dialogs, which cannot use the Signal colors.
- The grid view has no more button. Its menu opens with a long press.
- On touch screens, moving files works only by dragging.
- After a tap, the hover background can stay on the tapped row.
